# Leave Policy (SAMPLE - replace with your real documents)

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year. Leave above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Working from another country requires written approval from HR.

# Data & Security Policy

## Customer data
Customer personal data must never be copied to personal devices, personal email, or public AI tools.
Only company-approved tools may be used to process customer information.

## Expense reimbursement
Expenses above 5,000 INR need prior written approval from the department head. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.
